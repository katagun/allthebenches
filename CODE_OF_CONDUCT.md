# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Critique results and implementation choices rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and deliberate misrepresentation of benchmark evidence are not welcome.

Respect contributors' privacy and upstream authors' licenses. Disclose affiliations when they matter to a proposed evaluation or data correction.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation, proportionate to the conduct. To report an incident, contact a repository maintainer privately through a contact method they publish. Do not include another person's private information in a public issue. If no private channel is available, request one without posting incident details.
