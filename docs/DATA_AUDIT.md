# Dataset audit

**Snapshot reviewed:** 17 October 2026. **Curation status:** source-backed MVP seed; all observations in the curated seed are manually reviewed, but many are provider-reported and the data does not establish a common leaderboard. The canonical records are in [`data/curated/dataset.json`](../data/curated/dataset.json). Retrieved byte snapshots and hashes are retained as a local audit cache; verify source pages again before publication.

## Coverage

The curated snapshot contains 37 exact model releases, 309 benchmark variants, 663 reviewed observations, 36 serving routes, 36 route-specific price snapshots, and 19 recorded coverage gaps. It includes 67 exact model configurations. It spans all required high-level categories: general text/knowledge work, reasoning, mathematics, coding, agent tasks, and safety. “Vals Index” is broad knowledge-work evidence, not a dedicated instruction-following benchmark. Qwen AgentWorld is a specialized language-world-model evaluation and must not be read as a general assistant result.

The current requested families represented by a scored release are OpenAI (GPT-6 Astra), Anthropic (Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1), Google (Gemini 4 Argon), Meta (Muse Wonder Full), Mistral (Large 4 preview), and xAI (Grok 4.7). GPT-6.1 Sol, Muse Wonder base, and Muse Spark 1.3 now have exact sourced observations; no scores were inferred from related releases. Qwen and DeepSeek now include historical open-weight releases; DeepSeek headquarters remains unknown in the catalog. BullshitBench V2 adds three separately labeled metrics, each with ten exact scored model releases. These improve broad reliability coverage; the V1 variants and TRACES each have only three matched releases. The current default matrix gap remains explicit because the fourth shared column is not yet supported across the same six releases.

## Preset and interpretation

The Frontier preset includes ten configurations and the full set of 19 benchmark rows in Google’s Gemini 4 Argon evaluation table, including two separate GraphWalks context ranges. It retains the existing GDP.pdf and safety selections (Meta CyberBench and Lakera B3). Google’s reported competitor settings remain labeled with unresolved exact effort where the source does not identify an effort label. The initial view also retains its Meta attack-success and Mistral B3 columns. Historical releases, open-weight releases, Qwen3 base checkpoints, and DeepSeek reasoning are available through More views; each applies its own relevant benchmark columns. All models includes every curated configuration and benchmark, including all Arena leaderboard variants. Unscored models remain optional, and no preset asserts that all its columns share an evaluation protocol. See [`data/presets/`](../data/presets/) for the exact selections.

The table can show several observations for one model and benchmark when sources or setups differ. In particular, some reported benchmark values conflict across official sources (for example source-specific Terminal-Bench 4.0 rows). Keep them as separate evidence records with their own series and reporting relationships; do not select the largest value or paint a common-series winner without a later review decision. The preset does not settle those source conflicts.

## Explicit gaps and next review actions

- **Default matrix:** only three exact variants reach six distinct scored releases. Do not claim the four-column/six-model launch target is met until a fourth comparable or clearly labeled reported variant is curated.
- **Frontier coverage:** all requested families now have numeric evidence. GPT-6.1 core launch-chart values and some Meta safety/Mistral chart-only results remain unresolved; see the frontier review audit.
- **Mistral capability matrix:** Mistral Large 4 remains a preview. Nine explicit capability results are now curated alongside the two safety results; remaining charts and exact inference settings still require review.
- **DeepSeek:** an official 10 September changelog search result showed a V4.1-Flash score block, but direct retrieval timed out and the footnote uses the V4-Flash name. Confirm exact model attribution and units before creating observations.
- **Historical anchors:** GPT-4 now anchors MMLU (86.4%, 5-shot), GSM-8K (92.0%, 5-shot chain-of-thought), and HumanEval (67.0%, 0-shot) from its 2023 technical report. Selected early Claude/Gemini/Llama/Mistral/Mixtral releases are now curated with separate report/protocol variants. Coverage is still partial.
- **Lab headquarters:** seven labs have country codes from exact directory matches; DeepSeek remains unknown because the checked primary sources did not establish headquarters. The country reflects the lab/company headquarters claim in that directory, not the geographic location of all research teams.
- **Prices and OpenRouter usage:** current route prices are captured for six exact mapped releases (36 provider routes). Other curated releases do not have an exact current OpenRouter route in this snapshot. No usage rows are emitted because none of the 13 exact source IDs in the reconciliation proposal appears in the daily ranking feed, which publishes the top 50 variants plus an aggregate `Other` bucket. Omitted and absent rows mean unknown, never zero.
- **Source snapshots:** exact downloaded primary-source bytes are retained under [`data/curated/source_snapshots/`](../data/curated/source_snapshots/), with SHA-256 values where captured; newer manual report extractions retain versioned URLs and exact table locators rather than claiming cached byte hashes. Qwen's README is commit-pinned. These hashes identify retrieved bytes, not necessarily the rendered browser representation used for manual extraction.

## BullshitBench and TRACES integration

The curated snapshot now includes 75 BullshitBench observations from 25 exact model/reasoning rows in the pinned V1 and V2 CSV snapshots, plus 12 TRACES observations for three exact model identities. BullshitBench has six benchmark variants: average grade, clear-pushback rate, and accepted-nonsense rate for each separately versioned suite. V1 maps to three local releases per metric; V2 maps to ten. CSV clear and accepted rates retain their all-attempt denominator, and V1/V2 are not combined. The dedicated [BullshitBench preset](../data/presets/bullshitbench_oct2026.json) and [TRACES preset](../data/presets/traces_oct2026.json) keep each view scoped to its reviewed evidence.

TRACES records four distinct aggregate metrics from Intellicat's full-panel-10x result snapshot. Its three mapped releases are GPT-5.6 Sol, DeepSeek R1 Distill Qwen 32B, and Llama 3.1 8B Instruct. The model reasoning settings and endpoint-level routes are not disclosed, and the interval confidence level is not stated. See the [BullshitBench audit](../data/expansion/bullshit-benchmark-audit.md) and [TRACES audit](../data/expansion/intellicat-audit.md) for pinned source identities, extraction locators, metric meaning, and limitations.
